Privacy Policy

TSC Official, the app of Tabernacle of Solace Church. Last updated: 28 November 2026.

1. Who we are

Tabernacle of Solace Church ("TSC", "we", "us") is a church based in Ibadan, Nigeria. This policy explains what information the TSC Official app collects, why we collect it and how we protect it.

2. What we collect

You can read the devotionals, sermons, programmes, gallery, radio information and Bible reading plan without giving us any personal information. We collect information only when you choose to send it to us through a form:

  • Prayer requests: your name (or "Anonymous"), how you would like to be contacted, optional contact details and your request.
  • Membership registration and new believer forms: your name, phone number, email address, area, faith status, departments you are interested in, your message and the help you ask for.
  • Pastoral counselling requests: your name, phone number, optional email, how you want to be contacted and to meet, the topic and any message you write.
  • Testimonies: your name or "Anonymous", where you are from, your testimony and your permission to publish it.
  • Contact and invitation messages: your name, phone or email, the subject and your message.

Every form asks for your consent with a tick box before it can be sent. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or payment details through the app.

3. How we use it

  • To pray for you and to follow up on your request.
  • To contact you about membership, counselling, invitations and enquiries.
  • To keep church records and to understand how many people we are serving.
  • To publish your testimony, but only after our team has reviewed it and only if you gave permission.

4. Who can see your information

Information you send is visible only to TSC staff who have been given access for their role, such as the pastoral team for prayer, new believer and counselling requests. Pastoral counselling requests are limited to pastoral staff and the church's administrators. Finance records are separate and are not connected to the forms. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Where it is stored and how we protect it

Your information is stored in a secured online database provided by Supabase, and the app is hosted on GitHub Pages. These providers process data on our behalf. Information is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Visitors cannot read other people's submissions, and staff accounts are protected by sign-in and role-based access that we can switch off at any time.

6. What stays on your phone

If you use the Bible reading plan, your progress (the day you started and the days you marked as read) is saved only on your own phone. It is not sent to us.

7. Links to other sites

Some buttons open other websites, such as Bible Gateway for Scripture passages, TSC Radio, WhatsApp and our church website. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as it is needed for pastoral care and church records. You may ask us to delete it at any time.

9. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. To do this, contact us using the details below and tell us the name and phone number or email you used. We will respond as soon as we reasonably can. We aim to handle personal information in line with applicable data protection law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

10. Children

The app is for general church use and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
